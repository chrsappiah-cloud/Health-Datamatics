# App Review Response

Health-Datamatics is an analytics, informatics, and reporting workbench for healthcare operations. It provides dashboards, cohort summaries, data quality signals, explainable recommendations, and report-ready narrative sections.

Reviewer access:

- No login is required for the current review build.
- Launch the app and use the tab bar to review Dashboard, Patients, Quality, Insights, Reports, Integrations, and Architecture.
- The app currently uses sample/local data for demonstration.

Clinical safety:

- The app does not provide emergency guidance.
- The app does not replace clinician judgment.
- Insight recommendations are framed as explainable analytics suggestions with rationale, confidence, and rule traces.
- Any future deployment with real clinical data should be governed by the healthcare organization's privacy, security, consent, and clinical review procedures.

Data/privacy:

- The current starter build does not transmit live patient data.
- It does not include third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.
- If connected to production FHIR, HL7, warehouse, LLM, or vendor APIs later, privacy labels and review notes should be updated before submission.

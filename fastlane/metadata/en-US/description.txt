Health-Datamatics is an iOS-first healthcare analytics workbench for clinicians, analysts, and operations teams.

The app brings dashboard monitoring, patient cohort summaries, healthcare data quality checks, explainable insight workflows, and report-ready narratives into one focused mobile experience.

Core capabilities include:

Dashboard KPIs for readmissions, length of stay, quality performance, outcomes, and active alerts.

Patient and cohort views for longitudinal summaries, utilization patterns, observations, diagnoses, medications, and report context.

Data quality analysis for missingness, duplicate patient signals, coding gaps, broken references, outliers, timestamp inconsistencies, and incomplete observations.

Explainable insights that pair recommendations with rationale, confidence, affected measures, and transparent rule traces.

Report generation sections for executive summaries, cohort overviews, trends, risks, quality issues, intervention opportunities, and patient impact narratives.

Integration-ready architecture for SMART-on-FHIR, HL7/FHIR normalization, healthcare data warehouses, terminology services, and analytics backends.

Health-Datamatics is designed as a decision-support and analytics companion. It does not replace clinical judgment, emergency care, or authoritative medical record systems.
